Before the Workshop

Take 3–5 minutes to reflect on these questions. There are no right answers here — use them to prepare your thinking for the session.

Reflection 1
The course frames drug discovery as three big questions: What is broken? Can we reach it? Can we fix it well enough? Which of these feels most uncertain or interesting to you — and why?
Reflection 2
Think of a disease you know something about. At which stage of the value chain do you think most drug programs fail for that disease — and what kind of data would reduce that risk?
Reflection 3
RNA can be targeted earlier in the information chain than proteins. What do you think this changes about the question "Can we reach it?" compared to a small molecule targeting a protein?
Reflection 4
Sembragiline hit its target but failed in the clinic. Risdiplam succeeded by building a complete translational chain of evidence. What does this suggest about what "target validation" really means?
